[Your Name / Studio] ("we") operates Magic Sort. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address (if you sign in with email), and the name and email provided by Google or Apple if you use those options.
  • Game data: your display name, current level and last-updated time, which power the leaderboard.
  • Purchase data: product purchased and a transaction identifier, used to deliver your coins. We never see your card details.
  • Advertising and device data: ad identifier, IP address and device information collected by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • Diagnostics: basic crash and performance data.

How we use it

To run your account, save progress, show the leaderboard, process purchases, show ads, prevent fraud and improve the game.

Who we share it with

Service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, servers), Google AdMob (ads), Apple and Google (purchases). Your display name and level are visible to other players on the leaderboard. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account exists. You can delete your account and game data in Settings > Delete account. You can also email [support@example.com].

Your choices

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data; contact us to exercise them.

Children

Magic Sort is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact [support@example.com] and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method is perfectly secure.
