Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Deepam Sharma ("we") operates Pour Pop. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address (if you sign in with email), and the name and email provided by Google or Apple if you use those options.
  • Game data: your current level and progress, and the time it was last updated, so we can save and restore your progress.
  • Purchase data: product purchased and a transaction identifier, used to deliver your coins. We never see your card details.
  • Diagnostics: basic crash and performance data.

Pour Pop does not show ads and does not collect advertising identifiers.

How we use it

To run your account, save your progress, process purchases, prevent fraud, and fix bugs and improve the game.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, servers) and Apple and Google (purchases). They process data only to provide their services to us. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with other players.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account exists. You can delete your account and game data in Settings > Delete account. You can also email deepamsharma668@gmail.com to request deletion.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

Pour Pop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us at deepamsharma668@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
