Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Deepam Sharma ("we") operates Pour Pop. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Pour Pop has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, and we do not sell personal data.

Children

Pour Pop does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
